About TaskFlow

What is TaskFlow?

TaskFlow is a lightweight, browser-based task management application designed to help individuals and teams organize their work efficiently. It allows you to create, view, and manage tasks with priorities and deadlines.

Purpose

The purpose of TaskFlow is to provide a simple, clean interface for tracking daily tasks without the complexity of heavy project management tools. Whether you have one task or a hundred, TaskFlow keeps you on track.

How to Use TaskFlow

  1. Home / Dashboard: Start here to see an overview of all your tasks — total, pending, and completed counts.
  2. Add Task: Click "Add Task" in the navigation to create a new task. Fill in the title, description, due date, and priority level.
  3. Task List: View all your tasks in a table. Use the filter buttons (All / Pending / Completed) to narrow down your view.
  4. Task Detail: Click on any task to see its full details. You can mark it as complete or delete it from this page.
  5. About: You are here! This page explains the app and how to use it.

Navigation Guide

Use the navigation bar at the top of every page to move between sections. All pages are interconnected, so you can jump from any page to any other with a single click.
